Fan Adapter

This is used to suck fumes out of my 3D-printer to a more ventilated area. The adapter consists of the 3D printed rim, a box for electronics, a piece of filter wool and an old computer fan. A plastic tube is finally atached to the "funnel"-looking part.

Simple Jet Engine

I wanted to check the airflow coming out of my computer with a visual indicator and thought to build a miniature jet engine. The air flows from the bottom through the back propeller. The back propeller spins the shaft and therefore the front propeller. To make the rotation as smooth as possible, I put a skateboard bearing to the shaft.
