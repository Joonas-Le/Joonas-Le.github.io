Welcome to my webpage

Welcome to my small corner of the internet

It feels like everything on the internet these days seems to lose its personality and turn into the same grey mass. All the websites look and feel the same, and you can't tell bot accounts from real people. That's why I decided to turn into indie web development and make my own webpage. I created this webpage to gather some of the projects that I'm proud of, and also to show them to anyone who might be interested.

Almost everything found on this page is my own work, but the exceptions can be found in the References tab. Also, I have not used AI in my projects other than for proofreading my texts and some code snippets. I think I have dyslexia and I suck at coding, so please give me a break :D

The page is still under construction (and will probably remain so for a while), but I'll try to update it whenever I have free time. But still, feel free to browse the different tabs and their contents. And I'm well aware that I have shared quite a lot of information about myself through this site, but I can assure any hacker that my passwords have nothing to do with my interests or work.

DISCLAIMER

This website is designed to be used on a computer. Some features may not work or appear correctly on mobile devices. Additionally, this website has only been tested with Firefox, Chrome, MS Edge and Opera GX browsers, so I cannot guarantee that the site will work properly on any other browsers.
